Prime Hub V5.3 — Home UI Lock

This patch locks the customer home screen to the preferred compact layout:

Welcome card:
- Welcome, <name>!
- Prime Hub Store
- Premium digital products with fast delivery.
- Automatic crypto confirmation
- Manual Wallet, Binance & UPI approval
- Instant delivery after approval
- Order history and support

Buttons:
- Shop
- Wallet
- Orders | Reviews
- Referral | Loyalty
- Language | Support

Search, VIP, Recommendations, Profile and extra home buttons are not shown on the home screen.
The underlying features remain available through their commands/other screens.
